# Samsung Mobile Battery Capacities and Power Management

Energy efficiency in Samsung mobile devices combines advanced lithium-ion cell chemistry with intelligent power management integrated circuits (PMICs) and dynamic display refresh frequency regulation.

## Battery Capacity Generations Comparison
- **Galaxy S22 Series**:
  - Galaxy S22: 3,700 mAh (25W wired)
  - Galaxy S22+: 4,500 mAh (45W wired)
  - Galaxy S22 Ultra: 5,000 mAh (45W wired)
- **Galaxy S23 Series**:
  - Galaxy S23: 3,900 mAh (25W wired)
  - Galaxy S23+: 4,700 mAh (45W wired)
  - Galaxy S23 Ultra: 5,000 mAh (45W wired)
- **Galaxy S24 Series**:
  - Galaxy S24: 4,000 mAh (+100 mAh increase over S23; 25W charging)
  - Galaxy S24+: 4,900 mAh (+200 mAh increase over S23+; 45W charging)
  - Galaxy S24 Ultra: 5,000 mAh (unchanged capacity; 45W charging)

## Power Efficiency Gains Between Generations
While the Galaxy S24 Ultra maintains the same 5,000 mAh battery capacity as the S23 Ultra, its real-world battery endurance is extended by approximately 3 to 4.5 hours of continuous active use due to:
1. **Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 Efficiency**: TSMC N4P process node delivers up to 25% better power efficiency on CPU and 20% on GPU.
2. **LTPO 1-120Hz Panel Across All Models**: For the S24 series, variable refresh rate down to 1 Hz was extended to standard S24 and S24+ models (previously limited to Ultra on S22/S23), saving significant static display power during text reading and Always-On Display mode.
3. **Adaptive Battery Algorithm with Gauss AI**: Intelligently throttles background processes and optimizes network modem transmission frequencies according to individual user daily usage routines.
