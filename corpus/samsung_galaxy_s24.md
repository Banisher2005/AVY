# Samsung Galaxy S24 Series Specifications and Features

The Samsung Galaxy S24 flagship lineup consists of three devices: Galaxy S24, Galaxy S24+, and Galaxy S24 Ultra, launched in January 2024.

## Galaxy S24 Ultra Hardware Specifications
- **Display**: 6.8-inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X, Flat QHD+ resolution (3120 x 1440), 1-120Hz adaptive refresh rate, 2,600 nits peak brightness, Corning Gorilla Armor anti-reflective glass.
- **Frame Material**: Titanium frame providing increased rigidity, scratch resistance, and lightweight durability.
- **Processor**: Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 for Galaxy worldwide, manufactured on TSMC's 4nm node with custom prime core clocked up to 3.39 GHz.
- **Vapor Chamber Cooling**: 1.9x larger vapor chamber cooling system compared to the Galaxy S23 Ultra for sustained thermal stability during continuous gaming and neural workloads.
- **Battery**: 5,000 mAh battery supporting 45W wired charging (Super Fast Charging 2.0) and Fast Wireless Charging 2.0 (15W).
- **Cameras**: Quad rear camera system featuring a 200MP wide primary sensor with Optical Image Stabilization (OIS), 50MP periscope telephoto lens with 5x optical zoom and 10x optical-quality zoom (ProVisual Engine), 10MP telephoto with 3x optical zoom, and 12MP ultra-wide camera.

## Galaxy S24 and Galaxy S24+ Specifications
- **Galaxy S24**: 6.2-inch FHD+ Dynamic AMOLED 2X display, 4,000 mAh battery, 25W wired charging, Armor Aluminum 2.0 frame, equipped with Samsung Exynos 2400 in most international markets and Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 in select regions (US, Canada, China).
- **Galaxy S24+**: 6.7-inch QHD+ Dynamic AMOLED 2X display, 4,900 mAh battery, 45W wired charging, 12GB RAM standard, equipped with Samsung Exynos 2400 or Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 depending on geographical region.
- **Galaxy AI Features**: Circle to Search with Google, Live Translate for real-time bidirectional voice calling, Note Assist, Transcript Assist, and Generative Photo Edit.
