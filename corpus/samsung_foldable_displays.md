# Samsung Galaxy Z Fold and Z Flip Form Factors

Samsung Display and Mobile eXperience (MX) divisions lead the commercial foldable smartphone category through the Galaxy Z Fold and Galaxy Z Flip families.

## Flex Hinge Mechanism
The dual-rail teardrop 'Flex Hinge' allows the foldable panel to form a waterdrop-shaped curve inside the hinge housing when closed:
- **Zero Gap Closure**: Enables the two halves of the device to fold completely flat without a visible gap, significantly minimizing pocket dust ingress.
- **Crease Reduction**: Spreads the mechanical bending stress over a wider radius, visibly reducing the center screen crease compared to U-shaped hinges.
- **Flex Mode Angles**: The hinge holds its position freely between 75 and 115 degrees, enabling hands-free video conferencing, camera capture, and desktop-style split screen interfaces.

## Ultra Thin Glass (UTG) and Display Durability
- **Ultra Thin Glass (UTG)**: Engineered with a thickness of only 30 micrometers, providing glass-like scratch resistance and optical clarity while retaining high mechanical elasticity for over 200,000 fold cycles.
- **Armor Aluminum**: The outer housing and hinge cover are constructed from Samsung's proprietary high-strength Armor Aluminum alloy.
- **Water Resistance**: Devices feature IPX8 water resistance rating, certified for submersion in up to 1.5 meters of fresh water for up to 30 minutes.
