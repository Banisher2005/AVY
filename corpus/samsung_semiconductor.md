# Samsung Semiconductor Innovations & GAA Process

Samsung Electronics Foundry is a pioneer in advanced semiconductor manufacturing nodes. In June 2022, Samsung became the world's first foundry to announce commercial mass production of chips utilizing 3-nanometer (nm) Gate-All-Around (GAA) transistor technology, branded as Multi-Bridge-Channel FET (MBCFET).

## Gate-All-Around (GAA) vs FinFET
Compared to conventional FinFET architecture where the gate covers three sides of the channel fin, Samsung's MBCFET nanosheet implementation encases all four sides of the channel nanosheets. This design drastically reduces subthreshold leakage current, increases drive current per unit area, and provides flexible channel width customization.

Key generation metrics for Samsung's 3nm GAA compared to 5nm FinFET:
- Power consumption reduction: up to 45% lower power at equivalent clock frequencies.
- Performance enhancement: up to 23% higher performance at identical power draw.
- Surface area reduction: up to 16% smaller silicon footprint.

## Second-Generation 3nm (SF3) and Beyond
Samsung's second-generation 3nm node (SF3) introduces optimized nanosheet stacking and enhanced interconnect metallization. SF3 delivers an additional 20% power reduction and up to 30% performance density gain. Future roadmaps feature 2nm (SF2) production slated for mobile and high-performance computing (HPC) applications, integrating backside power delivery network (BSPDN) technology to eliminate frontside interconnect congestion.
